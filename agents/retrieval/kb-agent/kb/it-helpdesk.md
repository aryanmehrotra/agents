# IT Helpdesk

## VPN access
To connect to the corporate VPN, install the GlobalProtect client from the self-service portal and sign in with your SSO credentials. If you see "connection refused", your device certificate has likely expired — reboot once, and if it persists open a ticket with the Network team.

## Password reset
You can reset your own password at reset.internal.example.com. Passwords expire every 90 days. After five failed attempts your account locks for 30 minutes; contact the IT helpdesk to unlock sooner.

## New laptop / hardware requests
Hardware requests go through the IT portal under "Assets". Standard laptops are provisioned within 3 business days; specialized hardware (GPUs, extra monitors beyond two) needs manager approval and may take up to 2 weeks.

## Software installation
Approved software installs itself from the Company Portal app. For anything not listed, submit a software request ticket with a business justification. Admin rights are not granted on managed laptops.

## MFA / lost device
If you lose your MFA device, contact the IT helpdesk immediately to revoke the old device and enroll a new one. You will need to verify your identity with your manager on a video call.
