# HR Policies

## Leave and time off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 10 public holidays. Unused leave up to 5 days carries over to the next year; anything above 5 is forfeited on Dec 31. Sick leave is separate and does not count against annual leave.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers get 6 weeks. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Notify HR at least 8 weeks in advance where possible.

## Expense reimbursement
Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of purchase with an itemized receipt. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to $60/day. Personal travel upgrades are not reimbursable. Approved expenses are paid in the next payroll cycle.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully remote arrangements require director approval and a home-office stipend request. Core collaboration hours are 10am–4pm in your team's primary timezone.

## Referral bonus
If you refer a candidate who is hired and stays 90 days, you receive a referral bonus paid in the following payroll cycle. There is no limit on the number of referrals.
